Protected checkout

Cart, escrow, approval, payout.

Add multiple creators to one campaign cart. IVRE holds the payment and releases it only after the client approves the work and verifies the live post.

01

Buy creators

Brands checkout through IVRE with a platform fee on the creator order total.

02

Auto brief

Creator receives an automated first message asking for brand details, content type, script needs, and deliverables.

03

Accept or reject

Creators get 3-4 days to accept. Rejections trigger recommended replacements so the sale is not lost.

04

Corrections locked

Each creator sets correction limits while building the profile. Minimum one correction is compulsory.

05

Approve work

After correction limits are reached, approval is sent automatically and creator is asked to post.

06

Verify post

Creator submits the reel link. IVRE verifies it and client confirms before payout release.
